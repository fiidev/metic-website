# METIC - Moklet Education of Technology Informatic Club

![METIC Logo](https://www.moklet.org/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fmokletorg%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fv1721611310%2Fapp_media%2Ffile_u6zi7e.png&w=256&q=75)

METIC (Moklet Education of Technology and Informatics Club) is a technology organization at SMK Telkom Malang that serves as a space for students to explore, innovate, and collaborate in digital technology, creative business, and media communication.

This Project is Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma, PostgreSQL, and Cloudinary.

METIC has three main divisions, all actively creating real work and impact both at school and beyond.

- MokletDev - A division focused on technology development and innovation.
- MedCom - Focused on education, personal branding, and media partnerships.
- Metic Merch - A division focused on technology development and innovation.

With the spirit of Creating Youth Innovators, METIC shapes a generation of young leaders who are innovative, collaborative, and solution-oriented in the digital era.
